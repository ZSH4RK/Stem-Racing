#### Team Roles
These are the suggested roles from Stem Racing UK to be included in a team. However they are not set 1 person (except Project Manager). Multiple people can work across multiple jobs and people can double up on roles.

##### Project Manager
- Managing the team
- Project Management
- Deadline management

##### Finance Manager
- Organising budgets and resources
- Expense and income tracking
- Allocating Funds
##### Manufacturing Engineer
- Advising on manufacturing
- Constraints of machining process
- Working with Design Engineer to create the car

##### Design Engineer
- Aerodynamic performance creation
- Working with Manufacturing Engineer

##### Graphic Designer
- Responsible for colour schemes
- Car livery
- Graphical renderings of the car

##### Sponsorship & Marketing Manager
- Generating sponsorship proposals
- Creating and managing social media accounts
- Income generation ideas
