
## Judging Categories
###  Specification and Scrutineering
- Cars and replacement components are assessed for compliance with the technical regulations
- A4 Engineering Drawings
- A4 Renderings
- Quality of finish and assembly
#### Scrutineering 
Engineering Drawings
>First or Third-angle orthographic projection matching the final car and unrendered isometric view or similar. Additional views to show sufficient detail. Parts list/bill of materials. Excellent regulation compliance shown. Complete and ordered table of contents.

Rendering
>Multiple views. Perfect match to the final car including branding. Realistic environment and lighting High-end render technique

Quality of finish and assembly
>Exceptional, professional-quality finish with no visible defects. Precision assembly with seamless fit of all parts. Branding perfectly applied. The two cars are virtually identical in finish and build quality.
